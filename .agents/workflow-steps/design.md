Begin only after the requirements are locked and the existing system has been researched. Develop at least two genuinely different approaches wherever the solution space allows it. For each, explain how it fits the existing system, its complexity, coupling, migration and compatibility risks, what it forecloses, and how it can be rolled back. Do not present cosmetic variations as separate choices.

Recommend one approach and explain why it best satisfies the locked requirements and project canon. Where the operator rules, every material design fork goes to the operator. Where the agent rules, a fork that changes what the user sees, or product direction, still goes to the operator; the agent rules every other fork and records the ruling. This step is done only when an approach is chosen and every material design fork has a recorded ruling. Record the chosen direction and rejected alternatives for the task text when they remain useful to implementation.
