Settle what is required before discussing how to build it. Digest the supplied context, inspect enough of the current product to identify where the request lands, and separate observed behavior from assumptions. Draft a singular outcome, concrete requirements, verifiable acceptance criteria including rejection and error behavior, and explicit boundaries. Surface contradictions and unresolved questions.

Do not propose architecture or implementation choices in this step. Present the requirement draft and iterate with the requester. This step is done only when a recorded ruling locks the requirements and boundary.
