Read `{{report}}` and gather the report in the reporter's words. Establish what it touches by inspecting the relevant product surface, implementation, configuration, records, or other available evidence. Redact secrets and identifying information as it is gathered. Do not invent or silently resolve a fact that cannot be checked.

Record a concise issue restatement with these explicit parts: what happened; what was expected; how to reproduce it, including the command or action and environment; evidence; and what is not established. Distinguish the reporter's text from evidence produced by the investigation, and label an unexecuted reproduction as unverified.

This step is done when the complete restatement is recorded and every material claim is traceable either to the raw report or to named investigation evidence.
