Present every cohort with its signal count, live-work count, and closed-fix count. The operator chooses whether each cohort is worked and, for every selected cohort, whether it is one related group planned together or a set of unrelated fixes planned separately.

Do not regroup the signals or plan the work. This step is done only when every cohort is recorded as selected or not selected by an operator ruling. Each selected cohort then enters `plan-task` with its signals and record pointers; unrelated fixes enter separately.
