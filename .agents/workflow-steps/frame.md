State the question, change, or task in one sentence, then bound what this workflow will and will not resolve. Name the requester, the available evidence, the systems or records that may be read, and the condition that will make the work complete.

Record the frame before investigating. This step is done when the artifact distinguishes known facts from assumptions and names every material uncertainty that the investigation must settle.
