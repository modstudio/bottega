Present the investigation's findings, uncertainties, and implications to the requester. Answer follow-up questions from the recorded evidence, and investigate again when a follow-up exposes a material gap rather than guessing.

Record the answer reached, the question that remains open, or the reason no change should follow. This step is done when the requester has enough information to choose how the exploration closes and that disposition is preserved in the artifact.
