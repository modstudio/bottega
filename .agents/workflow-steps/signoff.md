Present the proposed outcome, boundaries, chosen design, work breakdown, dependencies, risks, and acceptance criteria together. Call out any assumptions or unresolved items rather than burying them in the plan.

This step is done only when the plan has an explicit recorded approval, or adjustments ruled on have been incorporated into it. Do not create or move task records on the strength of an unapproved draft.
