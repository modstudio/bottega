Present the proposed outcome, boundaries, chosen design, work breakdown, dependencies, risks, and acceptance criteria together. Call out any assumptions or unresolved items rather than burying them in the plan. Name `premise not checked` as an unresolved item for every requirement whose outcome evidence was not checked.

Where the operator rules, approval is the operator's recorded approval. Where the agent rules, record the agent's own approval with every unresolved item named; do not ask the operator to re-approve forks the agent was entitled to rule. This step is done only when the plan has an explicit recorded approval, or adjustments ruled on have been incorporated into it. Do not create or move task records on the strength of an unapproved draft.
