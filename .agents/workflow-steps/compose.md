Turn the framed subject and investigation evidence into the report the requester needs. Lead with the answer, distinguish observations from inference, cite each material source, and name every limitation or fact that could not be verified.

Keep recommendations within the requested scope and do not turn them into implementation. This step is done when a complete draft exists as a recorded artifact and every material claim traces to the investigation record.
