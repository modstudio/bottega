Present the complete issue restatement to the person who reported it and ask whether it accurately captures their report. Include what happened, what was expected, the reproduction and environment, the evidence, and what is not established so the ruling covers the body that will be filed.

This step is done only when the reporter explicitly confirms the restatement. If they correct it, revise the restatement and ask again before filing anything.
